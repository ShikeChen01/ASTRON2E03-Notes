Atmospheric Origin & Secondary Atmospheres

How terrestrial planets acquire, lose, and chemically sculpt their atmospheres — and how isotopic forensics tell the story.

What you're seeing

The canvas shows three columns — Venus, Earth, Mars — each with a horizontal bar proportional (log-scaled) to the selected atmospheric quantity. Numeric annotations appear next to each bar so you can compare orders of magnitude even when bars would be too short to see.

All three rocky planets outgassed similar CO2 budgets from their interiors. Earth's secret is the carbonate-silicate (Urey) cycle: rain dissolves CO2 into carbonic acid, which weathers silicate rock into carbonate minerals. Subduction returns that carbonate to the mantle; volcanism re-releases the CO2 — a long-period thermostat. Venus lost its liquid water early and therefore lost the cycle, leaving 92 bar of CO2 in its atmosphere. Mars lost both water and plate tectonics.

Isotopic forensics: Venus's D/H ratio is ~150× Earth's, because UV photodissociation of water preferentially allows light H to escape to space while deuterium (D) stays behind. Mars's D/H (~5× Earth) tells a smaller but similar story. Noble-gas ratios (36Ar/40Ar, 22Ne/20Ne) distinguish primordial nebular gas, outgassed interior volatiles, and late cometary/asteroidal delivery.

Three sources of secondary atmospheres: (1) Outgassing — volcanism releases CO2, H2O, N2, SO2 from the interior; (2) Late delivery — cometary/asteroidal impacts during the Late Heavy Bombardment (~3.9 Ga) contributed volatiles; (3) Primordial (nebular) — direct H2/He capture from the solar nebula, retained only by gas giants. Terrestrial planet atmospheres are therefore secondary: outgassed + delivered + chemically processed.

Try this

  • Click Show CO2 inventory: Venus has 92 bar, Earth 0.0004 bar, Mars 0.006 bar — yet the raw outgassed budgets were similar. The Urey cycle explains the difference.
  • Switch to Show H2O: Earth holds ~3000 m ocean equivalent. Venus and Mars are nearly dry today.
  • Switch to Show D/H: Venus's extreme enrichment (~100× Earth) is a fossil record of a lost ocean.
  • Toggle Show Urey cycle to see the carbonate-silicate loop that acts as Earth's long-term CO2 thermostat.
  • Switch to Show Ar isotopes: high 40Ar/36Ar on Earth signals intense outgassing from 40K decay; the pattern differs on Venus and Mars.
