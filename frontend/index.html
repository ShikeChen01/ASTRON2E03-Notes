1. Dynamics

  • Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion
    Ellipses, equal areas, and T² ∝ a³ — with a draggable eccentricity slider.
  • The Two-Body Problem
    Two masses orbiting their common center of mass — slide the mass ratio from 1:1 to the Sun-planet limit.
  • Conservation Laws
    Energy and angular momentum gauges over a Kepler orbit — KE and PE trade off while their sum stays flat.
  • Equation of the Orbit (Conic Sections)
    One equation, three shapes — slide eccentricity past 1 to watch ellipse → parabola → hyperbola.
  • Vis-Viva and Kepler III
    Speed depends on position via v² = GM(2/r − 1/a). Live velocity arrow + energy readout on the orbit.
  • Three-Body Problem & Chaos
    Three mutually-gravitating bodies. A tiny perturbation in initial conditions diverges exponentially.
  • Restricted Three-Body & Lagrange Points
    Five equilibrium points of the rotating frame — L4/L5 flip from stable to unstable at μ₂ = 0.0385.
  • Tidal Forces
    Why tides go as 1/d³, not 1/d² — bulges, the Roche limit, and a draggable distance slider.

2. Planetary Interiors

  • Moment of Inertia & Gravitational Figure
    Two-layer planet model — drag the core size and density ratio, watch C/MR² depart from the uniform value of 0.4.
  • Hydrostatic Equilibrium & Central Pressure
    The dP/dr = −ρg balance, P(r) parabola for a uniform sphere, and central-pressure scaling P_c ∝ M²/R⁴.
  • Differentiation & Dynamos
    Terrestrial vs giant interiors side-by-side, layered cross-sections, and where magnetic fields come from.
  • Planetary Heat Budget & Cooling
    Why small worlds cool fast — animated cooling, body-size slider, and the active-vs-cooled threshold at 4.6 Gyr.
  • Surfaces: Cratering, Volcanism, Tectonics, Erosion
    Power-law impact distribution; toggle the four geological processes; presets for Moon, Earth, Venus, Io.

3. Planetary Atmospheres and Thermal Balance

  • Blackbody Physics
    Planck curve, Wien's law, and Stefan–Boltzmann — drag temperature, watch the peak slide.
  • Equilibrium Temperature
    In = out for a planet. Slide distance, albedo, and stellar luminosity to see T_eq.
  • The Greenhouse Effect & Radiative Transfer
    Layer model — add IR-opaque layers and watch the surface temperature climb.
  • Hydrostatic Atmosphere & Scale Height
    P(z) = P₀ exp(−z/H). Slide T, g, μ and compare Earth, Venus, Mars, Jupiter.
  • Adiabatic Lapse Rate
    dT/dz = −g/c_p. A rising parcel cools; compare dry and moist lapse rates, test stability.
  • Cloud Formation & Phase Transitions
    Clausius–Clapeyron curves and where clouds form on Earth, Venus, Jupiter, and Titan.
  • Atmospheric Escape
    Maxwell–Boltzmann tail past v_esc. Slide T, m, g and see which species leak away.
  • Thermal Structure & Atmospheric Layers
    T(z) profile with tropopause, stratosphere, mesosphere, and thermosphere — Earth vs giant-planet profiles.
  • Winds: Coriolis, Hadley Cells, Zonal Bands
    Rotating frame deflects air parcels. Slide rotation rate and see Earth (3 cells) vs Jupiter (many bands).
  • Atmospheric Origin & Secondary Atmospheres
    Primordial vs outgassed vs delivered. Compare noble-gas and D/H signatures for Venus, Earth, Mars.
