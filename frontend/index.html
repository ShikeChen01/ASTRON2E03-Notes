Interactive notes for ASTRON 2E03.

1. Dynamics

  • Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion
    Ellipses, equal areas, and T² ∝ a³ — with a draggable eccentricity slider.
  • The Two-Body Problem
    Two masses orbiting their common center of mass — slide the mass ratio from 1:1 to the Sun-planet limit.
  • Conservation Laws
    Energy and angular momentum gauges over a Kepler orbit — KE and PE trade off while their sum stays flat.
  • Equation of the Orbit (Conic Sections)
    One equation, three shapes — slide eccentricity past 1 to watch ellipse → parabola → hyperbola.
  • Vis-Viva and Kepler III
    Speed depends on position via v² = GM(2/r − 1/a). Live velocity arrow + energy readout on the orbit.
  • Three-Body Problem & Chaos
    Three mutually-gravitating bodies. A tiny perturbation in initial conditions diverges exponentially.
  • Restricted Three-Body & Lagrange Points
    Five equilibrium points of the rotating frame — L4/L5 flip from stable to unstable at μ₂ = 0.0385.
  • Tidal Forces
    Why tides go as 1/d³, not 1/d² — bulges, the Roche limit, and a draggable distance slider.
